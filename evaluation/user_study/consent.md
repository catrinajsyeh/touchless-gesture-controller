# Consent script

Read this before a session. Do not start if the person says no.

You are being asked to try a class project that controls TikTok with hand poses seen by a small camera. You will also do the same steps with the keyboard. The session takes about ten minutes. You can stop at any time. Stopping will not affect your standing in the class.

The camera looks at your hand. We may keep the pose photos to improve the model. We will not record your face unless you separately agree. We will write down how long the task took and how many mistakes happened. Your name will not be in the report. A participant number is enough.

Do you want to take part?
